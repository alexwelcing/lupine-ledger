## Third-party data

Third-party datasets and upstream scientific sources keep their original
notices and licences, and CC BY 4.0 does not apply to them. This includes, for
example, reference values and potentials from OpenKIM, the NIST Interatomic
Potentials Repository, the Materials Project, MatPES, OMat24 and MPtrj, and
any published benchmark panels summarized in `src/reports/assets/`. Data we
derive from these sources keeps each item's original terms where those terms
require it. Check the upstream terms before reuse.
